Feature freeze

Every feature planned for the final build is in the live build as of Nov 18. From here on we only fix bugs, retune and polish, working from the playtest results below. The could-have items in the A5 schedule (adaptive signals, two-lane arterials, snow) are cut.

Release notes: changes since the Beta (A5)

  • Street life: people walk the pavements of every block, with legs and arms that swing as they walk. How many are out follows the time of day and the district (busy downtown and by the shops at midday, few at night), and fewer go out in rain, where they carry umbrellas. Parked cars fill the car parks of shops, offices and industry by day and the house driveways at night. Close to the camera they use the Car Kit model and further away the simple one, so street life costs about 5 FPS on Graphics high.
  • Street lights: pavement lanterns along every street besides the tall lamps. They come on one by one at dusk, and some come on by day in rain or fog. Each lamp lights a pool of light on the pavement.
  • Fires char buildings: a building on fire darkens the longer it burns, and stays charred after the fire is out.
  • End-of-shift call map: the result screen draws the city with the stations and every call of the shift (handled in time, late, or missed). Hovering over a call shows what it was and how it went.
  • Graphics levels: high, medium and low (button or G), remembered between visits. Medium drops shadows and birds and shows half the people. Low also drops clouds, shows a quarter of the people and uses the simple vehicle models. Measured uncapped in headless Chrome at the morning peak on our laptop: high 80, medium 93, low 119 FPS. This was before the walking people got limbs; the playtests re-measure it on other machines.
  • Sound and weather: a volume slider beside the mute button, remembered between visits. The shift menu has a weather choice (clear, rain, fog), and best scores are kept for each weather.
  • Playtest log (?playtest=S1): records clicks, keys, tutorial step times, every call and how long it waited, the frame rate every 5 s, any second under 40 FPS or freeze over 0.25 s with what was on screen, and the shift result. It stays in the browser until saved as JSON from a badge in the corner.
  • Stability: 84 automated tests (80 in the Beta), all passing.
