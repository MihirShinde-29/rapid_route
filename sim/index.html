Dispatch Alpha

Send ambulances, fire engines and police cars through live traffic. Cars pull over for sirens.

  1. A call comes in with a chime and a pop-up; off-screen calls get an arrow at the edge.
  2. Click the call (pop-up, pin, arrow or list). The panel lists the free units by ETA.
  3. Pick a unit and assign it, or press Enter to send the fastest. Tab goes to the next call.
  4. Reach calls before their target time for full points. A call nobody reaches before it expires is missed; too many lose the shift.
  5. Accidents block a lane and bring crash calls. C shows coverage, ? lists every key.

        Incidents

          None. Pick a kind, click Place on map, then click a lane. An accident blocks that lane for 20 sim min (during a shift, until a unit has dealt with it); a lane closure blocks a stretch of it (click its start, then its end). Cars get past through the oncoming lane, one side at a time. A road closure shuts the whole street. Cars heading that way reroute if a faster way exists.
